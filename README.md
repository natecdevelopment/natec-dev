# Natec Dev

Build a complete, premium real estate company website for Natec Development Ltd. based on the attached company profile document and 3D architectural renders.

In the hero/heading section, create an interactive scrollable construction animation effect where as the user scrolls, the building is constructed step by step (excavation, foundation, rising floor-by-floor frame, structural slabs, exterior facade installation, and the finished building from our 3D render images). Include ongoing construction visual effects, cranes/workers/machinery indicators, and floor progression counters.

Include the complete company profile:
- About Natec Development Ltd. (established 2023 in Sreepur, Gazipur, Bangladesh), Mission, Vision, and Core Values
- Services: Land Acquisition, Project Planning, Project Financing, Permits and Approvals, Building Design, Construction Management, Marketing and Sales, Property Management, Sustainable Development
- Projects showcase with status badges and details: Shobuj Angon (Completed 2023–2026 with our 3D building renders), Shobuj Aloy (Ongoing, 50% Complete), Shobuj Digonto (Ongoing, 20% Complete near Pepsi Gate), and Upcoming Projects planned for 2027
- Board of Directors profile cards (Chairman Md. Nazim Uddin, Managing Director Musa, Director Sanjida Akter, Director Mahamudul Hasan Khan, Director Shahin)
- Contact section with inquiry form, WhatsApp direct chat (+880 1990-665923), phone (01354-252400), office address at Rajendrapur Bazar, Sreepur, Gazipur, and location map
